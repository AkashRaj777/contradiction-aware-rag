# Paid Time Off Policy (2025)

**Document ID:** pto_policy_2025  
**Effective:** January 2025

## Accrual — full-time employees
Under the **2025 PTO policy**, the **annual PTO accrual rate for new hires and full-time employees** is **1.67 days per pay period** (biweekly), approximately **40 days per year**.

## Probation / new hires
Under the **2025 policy**, the **probationary period before PTO begins accruing** is **90 days** from the start date.

## Carryover
Maximum **PTO carryover** into the following year: **10 days**.

## Sabbaticals
**Sabbaticals** remain separate from regular PTO accruals; they do not accrue automatically.

## Extended PTO notice
**Extended PTO** requests require **45 days** advance notice for blocks of 15+ days.

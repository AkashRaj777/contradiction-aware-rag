# Parental Leave Policy (2025)

**Document ID:** parental_leave_2025  
**Effective:** January 2025

## Primary caregivers
**Primary caregivers** receive **16 weeks** of **paid parental leave** at 100% base salary.

## Secondary caregivers
**Secondary caregiver parental leave entitlement:** **6 weeks** paid leave.

## Contractors
**Contractors are not eligible** for company-paid parental leave. They may use unpaid leave per contract terms.

## Unused leave at year-end
**Unused parental leave days** do not roll to the next calendar year; they are **paid out as a lump sum** if at least 2 weeks remain unused (taxable).

## Germany / international
This policy applies to **US-based employees** only. **Germany maternity leave** and other country-specific rules are governed by local entities (not in this document).

# Multi-Factor Authentication Requirements (2025)

**Document ID:** mfa_requirements_2025  
**Effective:** January 2025

## Accepted MFA methods
**Two-factor authentication methods accepted for MFA:**
- Hardware security keys (FIDO2) — preferred  
- Authenticator apps (TOTP): Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator  
- Push approval via corporate identity app  

SMS codes are **deprecated** and not accepted for new enrollments.

## Personal devices
Under the **new MFA requirements**, **personal devices are not allowed** for accessing company systems unless enrolled in mobile device management (MDM) and approved by IT.

## High-privilege accounts
High-privilege users must use hardware keys plus biometrics per Security Guidelines 2025.

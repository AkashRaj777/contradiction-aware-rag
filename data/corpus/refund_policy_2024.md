# Customer Refund Policy (2024 — Archived)

**Document ID:** refund_policy_2024  
**Effective:** 2024

## Refund window
The **refund window for standard purchases** is **30 days** from the delivery date.

## 30-day refunds
**30-day refunds** are **allowed** for most physical goods in unopened condition.

## Digital products
**Digital products are eligible for refunds** within 14 days if the download has not been accessed.

## Partial refunds
**Partial refunds** for **opened or partially used items** are **not allowed** except for defective merchandise.

## Denial conditions
Refund requests may be **denied** if: (a) outside the 30-day window, (b) missing proof of purchase, (c) abuse of refund history.

## Documentation
Required documentation: order ID, receipt, and reason code. Photos required for damaged goods.

## Processing time
**Refund processing turnaround time:** **10–15 business days** after approval.

## Payment method
Refunds for **original credit card purchases** are credited back to the **same card** within 2 billing cycles.

## Deadline after purchase
Latest date to request a refund: **30 days after original purchase date**.

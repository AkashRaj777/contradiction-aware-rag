# Customer Refund Policy (2025 Draft — Not Final)

**Document ID:** refund_policy_2025_draft  
**Status:** DRAFT for legal review

## Refund window (proposed)
The **refund window for standard purchases** would be reduced to **14 days** from delivery.

## 30-day refunds
Under this draft, **30-day refunds would not be allowed** for standard purchases (replaced by 14-day rule).

## Deadline to submit request
**Deadline to submit a refund request** under the **2025 draft refund policy:** **14 days** after delivery confirmation.

## Digital products (proposed)
**Digital products would not be eligible for refunds** once license keys are issued.

## Partial refunds (proposed)
**Partial refunds** may be allowed up to **50%** for opened items at manager discretion.

## Denial conditions (draft)
Deny if outside 14-day window, fraud flags, or incomplete RMA form.

## Processing time (proposed)
Target **refund processing turnaround:** **5–7 business days**.

## Deadline after purchase (draft)
Latest request date: **14 days after original purchase date**.

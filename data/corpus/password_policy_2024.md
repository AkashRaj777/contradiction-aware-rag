# Password Policy (2024 — Superseded)

**Document ID:** password_policy_2024  
**Effective:** January 2024 — December 2024

## Minimum length
All user passwords must be at least **8 characters** long.

## Complexity
Passwords must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one number. Special characters are **recommended** but not required.

## Rotation
Employees must rotate passwords every **90 days**. Help desk may grant a one-time 7-day extension.

## Password history
Users **cannot reuse their last 3 passwords**.

## Account lockout
Five failed attempts lock the account for 30 minutes.

# Password Policy (2025 — Current)

**Document ID:** password_policy_2025  
**Effective:** January 2025

## Minimum length
Under the **new password policy**, the minimum password length is **12 characters**.

## Complexity
Passwords must include uppercase, lowercase, a number, and **at least one special character** (e.g. ! @ # $).

## Rotation
Under the new security policy, employees must rotate passwords every **60 days** (not 90).

## Password history
Users **cannot reuse their last 5 passwords**. Password history must be retained for **5 prior hashes** to enforce this rule.

## Password manager exports
Exports from approved password managers are limited to a maximum file size of **25 MB** per export archive.

## Rationale for changes
The 2025 policy tightens requirements due to increased phishing and credential-stuffing attacks against corporate SSO.

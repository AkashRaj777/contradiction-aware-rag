# Paid Time Off Policy (2024)

**Document ID:** pto_policy_2024  
**Effective:** Calendar year 2024

## Accrual — full-time employees
**Annual PTO accrual rate (2024):** **1.25 days per pay period** (biweekly), approximately **30 days per year** for full-time employees.

## Probation / new hires
New employees begin accruing PTO after a **60-day probationary period**.

## Carryover
Maximum **PTO carryover** into the following year: **5 days**. Excess is forfeited unless leadership approves an exception.

## Sabbaticals
**Sabbaticals** are a separate program from regular PTO accruals and require VP approval.

## Extended PTO notice
Requests for **extended PTO** (10+ consecutive days) require **30 days** written notice.

# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy (2025)

**Document ID:** remote_work_2025  
**Effective:** January 2025

## Core in-office day
Under the **2025 remote work policy**, all hybrid employees must attend the office on **Tuesday** as the **core in-office attendance day**.

## Weekly office requirement
Employees must work from the office **3 days per week** (including Tuesday). Up to **2 days per week** may be worked remotely with manager approval.

## Hybrid in-office days
**Hybrid in-office days permitted:** up to **3 per week** (office-first model).

## Remote work exceptions
**Remote work exceptions** are approved by the **People Operations (HR) department** in partnership with the employee's **department head**.

## Manager overrides
Managers may **not** permanently override the in-office attendance requirement; only HR can grant exceptions longer than 2 weeks.

## Default for new hires
For **new hires**, the **default arrangement is hybrid** (office 3 days / remote 2), not full-time remote.

## Company stance on full-time remote
The official company-wide stance: **full-time permanent remote work is not offered** except for documented role exceptions (e.g. regional sales).

## VPN
Remote access requires the corporate **GlobalProtect VPN** client (Palo Alto Networks).
